“Overemployment” refers to when people have more than one job. While previously the term referred to working more hours than necessary for financial reasons, many people are now technically over-employed while still working a 40-hour work week. This is becoming increasingly popular since many workers are realizing that they don’t need 40 hours to do their BS “40-hour-per-week job.” People may engage in over-employment to supplement their income or achieve specific financial goals, such as buying a house or saving up for crazy vacations.

In other words, many remote workers and digital nomads have traded the water cooler and surfing the web for more real work that pays real money.

There are several ways people can use over employment to make extra money, including:

- Taking on a part-time job in addition to their full-time job.
- Freelancing or doing gig work in their spare time.
- Starting a side business or monetizing a hobby.
- Renting out their assets, such as a spare room or a car, on sharing economy platforms.
- Offering their skills and services on freelance websites or marketplaces.
- Teaching things to people far far away.
- Providing functional solutions in an area of expertise.

It’s important to note that over-employment can also have serious negative consequences, such as burnout, fatigue, and reduced productivity. It’s also possible to get caught, and the ethics on this are blurry.

It is essential to balance earning extra income with maintaining a healthy work-life balance and ensuring that additional work doesn’t interfere with primary job responsibilities, lest the main gig squeeze finds out about the gig mistresses and asks for a career divorce.

## The Benefits of Overemployment

Over employment can offer many amazing benefits including:

### Extra income

Overemployment allows individuals to earn additional income, which can be used to pay off debt, save for future goals, or improve their overall financial situation. Most people will gladly receive extra money for the amount of work.

### Skill development

Taking on additional work can provide opportunities for individuals to learn new skills or develop existing ones, which can lead to career advancement in the primary gig. Content creators who take on a second job teaching courses on consumer behavior are bound to learn something new.

### Flexibility

Many types of over-employment, such as freelancing or gig work, offer flexibility in terms of when and where work is done, which can be convenient for individuals who have other commitments or responsibilities. This becomes even more interesting when leveraging time zones — I love it when I’m responding to my client's needs at 5 am local time.

### Networking

Over-employment can provide opportunities to meet new people and expand professional networks, which can be beneficial for career growth and development, and lead to more and better-paid normal employment

### Diversification

Having multiple sources of income can provide a sense of security, as it reduces reliance on a single source of income and helps individuals weather financial emergencies or downturns. If the primary gig cheats on you or leaves you, at least you’ll always have your side piece(s).

## How Digital Nomads Use Overemployment

Digital nomads use tech to work remotely while traveling the world or living in locations otherwise unattainable. For example, I live in France in a town that has absolutely nothing to do with my primary job. Over-employment can be a useful strategy for digital nomads to earn extra income and support their travel lifestyle, in several ways:

### Freelance Writing

Many digital nomads work as freelancers, offering their skills and services to clients around the world. Taking on additional freelance work can help them earn extra income and expand their portfolio of clients for future gigs. Writing in English is actually a marketable skill outside of English-speaking countries, so taking on projects of all types is common.

### IT Consulting

With the increasing reliance on technology, IT consultants are in high demand to help companies manage their IT systems, develop software solutions, and optimize their technology infrastructure. People who have functional expertise in a given area might be surprised to discover that they know more about deploying a new SaaS platform than the developers who created it.

### Teaching

Some digital nomads teach English, or teach other subjects online *in English*, which can provide a steady source of income and the flexibility to work from anywhere. This works especially great in Europe for all topics of business including marketing, finance, sales, and of course, IT.

### Affiliate marketing

Digital nomads who have a blog or social media following may be able to earn extra income through affiliate marketing, where they promote products or services and receive a commission for any sales generated. Bloggers can earn money through affiliate marketing, sponsored posts, and advertising — according to a 2021 survey by [Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/), the median annual income for bloggers is $11,000, but top earners can make six-figure incomes. Even the Median is a good boost as a side hustle.

YouTube creators can also earn money through advertising revenue, brand deals, and merchandise sales. The highest-earning YouTube creators can make millions of dollars per year, but smaller creators may earn a few hundred dollars per month with just a few hours of work.

Podcasters can earn money through sponsorships, advertising, and merchandise sales. According to a 2021 survey by [Podcast Insights](https://www.podcastinsights.com/), the top 1% of podcasters earn more than $50,000 per episode, while the median podcast earns less than $1,000 per year.

Overall, the amount of extra money content creators can earn via affiliate marketing, depends upon factors such as the type of content they create, the size of their audience, and the platforms they use to monetize their content, but extra money is extra money no matter.

### Business Consulting

Business consultants are also in high demand as companies seek to improve their operations and profitability. Business consultants can provide advice on strategy, marketing, operations, finance, and other areas such as what IT systems would be good to implement, and which are not.

## How to find international consulting jobs where you can work remotely

### Consulting firms

Many consulting firms offer remote work options for their employees. You can visit the websites of consulting firms that interest you and check if they have any remote job openings. Scouring LinkedIn for the smaller and more bite-sized firms is a good idea, since their hiring practices are oftentimes less arduous to navigate, but they still have the capability to secure a healthy budget to pay you with.

### Freelance websites

There are several freelance websites that allow you to create a profile and bid on consulting projects. Some popular freelance websites include Upwork, Freelancer, and Guru.

### Networking

Networking can be a powerful way to find remote consulting jobs. You can attend industry events, connect with professionals on LinkedIn, and join industry-specific groups to build your network. Online job boards and forums can be good places to do this — there are many online job boards and forums that specialize in remote work such as Remote.co, We Work Remotely, and Working Nomads.

## The Dangers of Overemployment

### Burnout

Overemployment can lead to burnout, which is a state of emotional, physical, and mental exhaustion caused by prolonged stress. When someone is over-employed, they may not have enough time for rest and self-care, which can lead to burnout and a decline in physical and mental health.

### Decreased productivity

When someone is over-employed, they may not be able to devote enough time and energy to each job or task, which can lead to decreased productivity and quality of work.

### Strained relationships

Overemployment can take a toll on personal relationships, as the person may not have enough time to spend with friends and family, leading to feelings of isolation and loneliness.

### Legal and financial risks

Taking on too many jobs or side hustles can lead to legal and financial risks, such as tax issues or breaches of contracts. It’s important to research and understands the legal and financial implications of each job or side hustle before accepting it.

### Career stagnation

Overemployment can prevent someone from focusing on their primary career or goals, leading to career stagnation and missed opportunities for growth and advancement.

## Putting it all together

It is very possible for many to take side hustle jobs internationally. With advances in technology and the increasing popularity of remote work, many side hustle jobs can be done from anywhere in the world, as long as you have an internet connection. Regardless of the method you use to find remote consulting jobs, it’s important to carefully review job descriptions, research companies, and tailor your application materials to each opportunity to increase your chances of success.

It’s important to find a balance between work and other aspects of life to avoid the potential dangers of over-employment. This may involve setting boundaries, prioritizing self-care, and being selective about the jobs and side hustles taken on.
